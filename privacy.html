Privacy Policy

Walent Family Archive Backup is a personal-use backup application operated by the owner of a private family archive.

Google Drive Access

The application uses Google Drive authorization to create, store, retrieve, and manage encrypted archive backup files in the owner's Google Drive account.

Data Storage

Backup files are encrypted locally before being uploaded to Google Drive. The application does not make the family archive publicly accessible.

Data Sharing

Google Drive data accessed by the application is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with independent third parties. Google Drive is used as an off-device backup storage provider.

Authorization

The owner may revoke the application's Google Drive access through their Google Account security settings. Stored backups can be deleted by the owner.
